1 · Everyone has a question

"Did anything really change where I live?"

Is there really less snow in the mountains? Is the soil drying out on our farm? Are summers in my city hotter? Has the rain changed since I was born? Which province warmed the most? Is the air getting hazier?

The answers are in NASA's data. But reading them takes three things most people do not have:

Thousands of files

Strange units

kg/m², Kelvin, kg per second: no everyday meaning.

3 · One answer, honestly

Three questions, three plain answers.

  • What is happening? One phrase: "Getting warmer".
  • How much? +0.42 °C per decade, or +1.9 °C in total: one switch.
  • How sure? Everyday words instead of p-values: "the chance this is a fluke is less than 1 in 1,000".

If we are not sure, we say so. Same place, different periods:

No clear change

1980–2011: no direction, no number.

Likely change

1980–2016: +0.22 °C / decade.

Clear change

1980–2025: +0.42 °C / decade, three checks agree.
