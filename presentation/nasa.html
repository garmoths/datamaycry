Our solution

Three steps to an answer.

  1. 1
    Pick a place

    Click the globe or search: continent, country, province, or one 55 km grid cell. 4,861 places.

  2. 2
    Pick what you wonder about

    Temperature, rain, humidity, wind, snow, soil moisture, evaporation, runoff, haze.

  3. 3
    Read the answer

    "Getting warmer: +1.9 °C over 1980–2025", and how sure we are.

One answer, honestly

  • What is happening? One phrase: "Getting warmer".
  • How much? +0.42 °C per decade, or +1.9 °C in total: one switch.
  • How sure? Everyday words instead of p-values; a level only when the 95 % range excludes zero.
No clear change

1980–2011: no direction, no number.
